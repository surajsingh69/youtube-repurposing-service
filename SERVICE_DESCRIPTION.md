# YouTube Content Repurposing Service

## What This Service Offers

I turn long YouTube videos into multiple structured content drafts:

- **Blog Drafts** - Draft articles with overview, key points, and sections
- **Twitter/X Thread Drafts** - Draft tweets selected from video content
- **Key Quotes** - Notable quotes with timestamps for social media
- **Summaries** - Condensed summaries drawn from the video transcript
- **Cleaned Transcripts** - Full text with artifacts removed
